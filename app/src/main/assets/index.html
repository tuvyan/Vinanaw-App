      About this app

      This free app contains the working Vinanaw–English dictionary compiled with the Vanaw community, the Vanaw Learner’s Guide, and simple lessons for learners. It works fully offline: once it is on your phone or computer, no internet connection is needed.

      Spelling

      Vinanaw has a sound not found in English or Filipino, an interdental approximant, written here as L̲ l̲ (an underlined l). Earlier materials sometimes wrote it as ɏ or r. When searching, you can type a plain l — the app will also find words spelled with l̲ — or tap the l̲ button to type it exactly.

      Other letters: j and v represent sounds that some related writing systems spell d and b; an apostrophe or hyphen marks a glottal stop (e.g. a-ayyon); stress is shown in the pronunciation line (e.g. ab.pál̲).
